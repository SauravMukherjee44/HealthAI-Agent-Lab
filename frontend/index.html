Research methodology

One language reasoner, many bounded tools.

Qwen will be confined to structured extraction, routing and explanation. Diagnostic scores remain the responsibility of versioned specialist models and deterministic calculators.

  • Extraction over invention: schema-constrained JSON only.
  • Deterministic execution: tool inputs are validated before inference.
  • Traceability: result, version, dataset and limitations travel together.

Safety is a control plane.

Emergency pre-emption

Severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, facial drooping, arm weakness or loss of consciousness bypass all models. Call India emergency services at 112.

verified_user

Bounded outputs

Every screening result states that it can be wrong and cannot establish or rule out a condition.

policy

Tool allowlist

visibility

Human confirmation

Voice transcripts and structured input values must be reviewed before any specialist model runs.

warning

Known limitations

  • Heart, diabetes, kidney and liver research routes are locally callable; none is clinically validated.
  • Models were trained on small public datasets and are not externally validated medical devices.
  • English voice transcription is locally verified; multilingual ASR remains future work.
  • Qwen handles conversation and constrained route proposals; deterministic code retains execution authority.
