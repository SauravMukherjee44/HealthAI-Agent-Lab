Research methodology

Open weights, bounded authority.

The language model can structure, ask and propose. It cannot silently invent measurements, execute an unregistered tool or convert a benchmark score into a diagnosis.

01Extraction over inventionSchema-constrained proposals preserve unknown values as unknown.
02Specialists over one giant modelNumerical, language, speech and imaging artifacts remain independently testable.
03Provenance travels with outputVersion, dataset, threshold and limitations accompany every result.
04Fail closedMissing artifacts, malformed fields or blocked capabilities cannot execute.

warning

Known limitations

  • Heart, diabetes, kidney and liver routes are research classifiers; none is clinically validated.
  • Small public benchmarks can contain source artifacts, imbalance and population shift. Heart and liver specificity are currently weak.
  • Perfect renal benchmark discrimination is a warning to investigate leakage and transportability—not evidence of clinical perfection.
  • PulmoVision uses pediatric 28×28 PneumoniaMNIST images and is not a general chest-radiology model.
  • HealthAI Voice 1.0 is English-only; transcripts can be wrong and require user review.
  • Rate limits constrain cost and casual abuse but cannot guarantee protection from every distributed denial-of-service event.
